    Proposed measurement definitions

    Activation denominator: consented accepted signups in a dated invitation cohort; report completion within seven days and time to first accepted reciprocal message. Return: D1/D7/D30 meaningful actions by signup cohort, plus week-four participation among eligible cohorts with a full observation window.

    Conversation participation: distinct consented account identifiers with accepted contributions, reciprocal exchanges and returning contributors; report room density and founder-host involvement. Payment: settled cash payers / exposed eligible members; distinguish earned tokens, token spend, requests and approved requests from collected revenue. Include sample sizes, missing events, dates, failures, refunds and safety outcomes.
