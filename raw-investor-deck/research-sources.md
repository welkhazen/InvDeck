# raW — sources and claim register

Prepared 17 October 2026. This is a provenance register, not a claim that every linked primary source was freshly verified. See [evidence status](evidence-status.md) for product status and unresolved investor claims.

## Verification method

The main-app source and aggregate Tribe Test runtime payload were freshly inspected at immutable commits. No raw participant histories were exported. The supplied PDF, *The Rise of Anxiety and Mental-Health Need: Global and Lebanon Investor Research* (states “Evidence current through 16 October 2026”), was read as supplied secondary research. Page references below refer to the PDF's printed pages.

The [previous research register](../next-social-wave/research-sources.md), compiled 16 October 2026, was inspected for existing WHO, Lebanon and digital-access sourcing. Its claim that earlier search summaries were checked is historical provenance, not verification performed in this session. Current direct primary-source requests to WHO, Frontiers, IHME and Reddit were blocked with HTTP 403. External URLs are cited as source destinations; successful resolution and current contents were not confirmed here. The source register therefore retains explicit supplied/inherited status rather than upgrading assertions to newly verified facts.

The supplied PDF's overall “94% confidence” and similar percentage scores have no demonstrated calibration and are not used. Its broad mental-health commercial recommendations are not raW's business thesis.

## Business research still required

The defensible opportunity is consumer social discovery, communities, identity and optional self-discovery. A bottom-up scenario should state eligible reachable audience × activation × retained participation × payer rate × net revenue per payer, with explicit time windows and no double-counting. Until measured, each input is an assumption rather than market evidence.

| Research / experiment | Evidence needed before a stronger claim |
|---|---|
| Competitor comparison | Review current official feature, pricing, age-policy and safety pages for Reddit, Discord, Instagram, TikTok, Yubo, Wizz, pseudonymous discussion apps, personality-test products and AI companions. Compare the user job, discovery trigger, identity model, community supply, monetization and safety. No “no competitors” claim. |
| Category economics | Primary company filings or verified platform disclosures, with matched reporting periods and definitions. [Reddit investor results](https://investor.redditinc.com/financials/quarterly-results/default.aspx) was blocked in this session; the [previously cited FY2024 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1713445/000171344525000018/rddt-20241231.htm) is a research lead, not newly checked evidence or a raW projection. |
| Lebanon/MENA payment access | Check Global Findex microdata definitions, local processor coverage, card/mobile-wallet access, cash settlement, fees, FX, refunds and native billing requirements. [Findex catalog referenced previously](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/7860) remains an unverified lead here. Account ownership alone is not digital-payment usage or willingness to pay. |
| Activation and retention | Server-accepted poll, join and message events; source attribution; dated signup cohorts; first reciprocal conversation; D1/D7/D30 action-based return; sample size and observation windows. No measured results presently supplied. |
| Paid demand | Observe package views, requests, approvals, actual cash settlement, token spends, refunds and repeat purchase. Separate paid tokens from rewards, and report fulfillment/support cost. An offered price is not realized revenue. |
| Discovery advantage | Compare answer-based routing against a simple community directory; measure join, reciprocal response and later return, with consent and a predetermined analysis window. |
| Regional expansion | Validate localization, moderator coverage, payment feasibility and distribution economics in each target community. Lebanon → MENA → international is a staged hypothesis, not achieved market entry. |

## Publication checks

Before circulation, an authorized reviewer should open the primary research pages, capture publication dates and the relevant methodology, resolve any discrepancy with this register, and confirm every displayed number has the same scope and unit. Research can remain concise without a misleading point estimate. Product code is inspected evidence; production behavior, business performance and human impact require separate measurements. No claim in this register establishes that raW treats depression, anxiety or loneliness.
