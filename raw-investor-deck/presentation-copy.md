## 08 · The helped become the helpers.

Someone arrives looking for common ground.
One day, they create it for someone else.

CONCEPTUAL CONNECTIONS · NOT MEMBER ACTIVITY

Proposed sequence: enter → find understanding → build confidence → contribute → welcome the next person. No causal retention or well-being outcome is established.

1. Enter with a little curiosity.

Someone arrives with an answer, not a finished public identity. Measure: accepted signup → first room entry.

2. Find a moment of understanding.

A relevant response gives the first exchange a reason to continue. Measure: first reciprocal conversation and voluntary experience feedback.

3. Build the confidence to participate.

A useful room makes the next visit feel easier. Measure: meaningful cohort return, alongside safety outcomes.

4. Become a contributor.

Returning participants offer their own perspective and keep a conversation going. Measure: returning contributors and reward-independent participation.

5. Welcome the next person.

The person who once needed an opening now creates one for someone else. Measure: member-led welcomes, reciprocal replies and founder-independent activity.

Proposed measurement definitions

Activation denominator: consented accepted signups in a dated invitation cohort; report completion within seven days and time to first accepted reciprocal message. Return: D1/D7/D30 meaningful actions by signup cohort, plus week-four participation among eligible cohorts with a full observation window.

## 14 · Make the next decision an evidence decision.

Proposed sequence, not committed delivery dates. Set numeric targets after a reliable baseline. Mobile/iOS readiness depends on technical, safety and store review; approval is not promised.

M01–02 · Establish a trustworthy baseline.

Verify accepted writes across signup, avatar, answer, room and message. Reconcile live versus seeded poll counts, confirm consent and test the reporting path. Acceptance evidence: a reproducible end-to-end journey, production migration checks and reconciled events with failures visible.

M03–05 · Observe three monthly cohorts.

Keep recruitment small and attributable. Describe windows and denominators; record first reciprocal conversations, return, room density and host effort. Acceptance evidence: three dated cohort readouts with sample sizes, event completeness and safety review.

M06–08 · Test the doorway against a baseline.

Compare opt-in answer-based room routing with a simple directory. Track join, reciprocal response, later meaningful return and member contribution. Acceptance evidence: an interpretable comparison with cohort limits, founder involvement and quality feedback disclosed.

M09–10 · Follow intent through to settled value.

Test one clear paid offer with secure payment and fulfillment. Separate earned tokens, requests, approvals and collected cash. Acceptance evidence: reconciled settlement, conversion denominator, refunds, repeat purchase and fulfillment costs.

M11–12 · Decide whether the evidence earns expansion.

Review retention, conversation usefulness, moderation burden, acquisition effort and unit economics. Re-scope where needed; do not equate feature delivery with demand. Acceptance evidence: a documented proceed, iterate or pause decision, with mobile and next-market readiness assessed.
