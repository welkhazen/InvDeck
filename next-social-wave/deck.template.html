    Definitions. Loneliness is the painful feeling that one's connections fall short of what one wants. Social isolation is having few connections. Neither is a clinical diagnosis. Gallup's separate global measure found 23% of adults felt lonely "a lot of the day yesterday" in 2023.2

    Limits. The 871,000 figure is a modeled association, not a count of deaths caused by loneliness. Prevalence estimates pool surveys that differ in wording and year.

    What raW is not. raW is a social discovery product. It is not therapy, a medical service or a treatment for loneliness, depression or anxiety.

    How to read these numbers

    Earlier and latest measurements for poverty and unemployment come from the same source and method, so the change is meaningful. The poverty figure uses a new, unofficial 2022 poverty line and excludes three governorates the surveyors could not reach.4

    Mental-health figures are not a trend. An earlier national survey (2002–2003, face-to-face diagnostic interviews) estimated lifetime prevalence near 25%. It used a different method, so the two numbers cannot be compared as before and after.3

    Correlation, not causation. These indicators show pressure on daily life. They do not show that social media or the lack of it caused any of it, and they do not show that raW would change them.

      08The Cumulative Mind

      raW didn't start with an algorithm. It started with people answering honestly.

      Before the app, the founder ran The Cumulative Mind, an Instagram account that asked one honest question at a time. Those answers became raW's first dataset. Here is each step from story poll to model, and how much of it is verified.

        Measured

        An adaptive model built from historical answers, with documented code, validation and limitations. It ships today in the Tribe Test.

        Suggested

        People's answers form stable clusters, and a short adaptive sequence of questions can place a new person near one of them.

        Not yet verified

        That the clusters predict who people will enjoy talking to, or that they improve retention in the app. That needs live product data.

        Reconciling the counts

        Three different vote counts appear in raW's records. 33,486 confirmed votes in the original extraction set. 37,302 trusted user-vote records in a later founder-reported audit of 105 poll instances (95 trusted, 10 awaiting review). 23,424 answers bridged from the audited master into the model across 31 polls. They come from different pipeline stages and dates, so they should not be added together.

        All three count vote records, not people. The clearest people count is 1,795 voters with at least eight answers, the group used to define the tribes.

        The audit's source files (the poll source of truth spreadsheet and database, the Poll Data Hub) were not available for this review, so the audit figures are shown as founder-reported. Instagram follower and per-question vote counts are also founder-reported.

        Data rights. The historical answers come from Instagram story polls. Usernames are kept in a private vault and never leave the founder's machine. Confirming the right to use this data in the product is on the legal checklist this round funds.

        On defensibility. A dataset alone is not a moat. The potential moat is a retained community that keeps answering, because each answer improves the questions and rooms that bring people back.
