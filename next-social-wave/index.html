    Definitions. Loneliness is the painful feeling that one's connections fall short of what one wants. Social isolation is having few connections. Neither is a clinical diagnosis. Gallup's separate global measure found 23% of adults felt lonely "a lot of the day yesterday" in 2023.2

    Limits. The 871,000 figure is a modeled association, not a count of deaths caused by loneliness. Prevalence estimates pool surveys that differ in wording and year.

    What raW is not. raW is a social discovery product. It is not therapy, a medical service or a treatment for loneliness, depression or anxiety.

    How to read these numbers

    Earlier and latest measurements for poverty and unemployment come from the same source, so the direction of change is meaningful. The January 2022 labour figures come from a phone follow-up of the 2018–19 sample (57.6% response). The poverty figure uses a new, unofficial 2022 poverty line and excludes three governorates the surveyors could not reach.4

    Mental-health figures are not a trend. An earlier national survey (2002–2003, face-to-face diagnostic interviews) estimated lifetime prevalence near 25%. It used a different method, so the two numbers cannot be compared as before and after.3

    Correlation, not causation. These indicators show pressure on daily life. They do not show that social media or the lack of it caused any of it, and they do not show that raW would change them.

      Keep in mind

      • The bars show the model's belief given your answers. They are not a measure of accuracy and not a personality diagnosis.
      • The eight tribes are clusters found in the historical answers of 1,795 voters (Instagram accounts). They are not externally validated personality types.
      • The full Tribe Test recovers a voter's data-defined tribe 87.9% of the time in an internal simulation on 1,780 voters, measured at a 27-question cap (uniform chance is 12.5%; always guessing the largest tribe would score about 28%).       08The Cumulative Mind

      raW didn't start with an algorithm. It started with people answering honestly.

      Before the app, the founder ran The Cumulative Mind, an Instagram account that asked one honest question at a time. Those answers became raW's first dataset. Here is each step from story poll to model, and how much of it is verified.

        Measured

        An adaptive model built from historical answers, with documented code, validation and limitations. It is built into the Tribe Test on theraw.me; live use is not yet verified.

        Suggested

        Tribe centres held steady as the data grew, and the test recovers most labeled voters' tribes from their own recorded answers. Voters near tribe boundaries moved between refits, and placing new people has not been tested.

        Not yet verified

        That the clusters predict who people will enjoy talking to, or that they improve retention in the app. That needs live product data.

        Reconciling the counts

        Three different vote counts appear in raW's records. 33,486 confirmed votes in the original extraction set. 37,302 trusted user-vote records in a later founder-reported audit of 105 poll instances (95 trusted, 10 awaiting review). 23,424 answers bridged from the audited master into the model across 31 polls. They come from different pipeline stages and dates, so they should not be added together.

        All three count vote records, not people. The clearest people count is 1,795 voters with at least eight answers, the group used to define the tribes.

        The audit's source files (the poll source of truth spreadsheet and database, the Poll Data Hub) were not available for this review, so the audit figures are shown as founder-reported. Instagram follower and per-question vote counts are also founder-reported.

        Data rights. The historical answers come from Instagram story polls. Usernames stay in private files on the founder's computer; this deck and the published model hold only aggregate statistics. Confirming the right to use this data in the product is on the legal checklist this round funds.

        On defensibility. A dataset alone is not a moat. The potential moat is a retained community that keeps answering, if each new answer is fed back into the questions and rooms that bring people back. That loop is not built yet: today the model is trained only on historical Instagram answers.

        10How the model can become a business

        Monetization follows meaningful participation.

        1. Stage 1

          Free participation

          A daily set of polls, open rooms and chat are free. Extra polls, locked rooms and the in-app Tribe Test cost tokens, which members can also earn.

          Verified in code
        2. Stage 2

          Premium identity

          Rare avatars and ranks, built and priced in tokens. There is no in-app checkout for tokens.

          Verified in code
        3. Stage 3

          Private and paid rooms

          Revenue hypothesis. Locked tribe rooms are built and can charge tokens on entry. Member-run rooms and cash payment are not built.

          In progress
        4. Stage 4

          Opt-in insights

          A fuller reading built only from a person's own answers, only if they ask for it. A preview is built; reports are not generated yet and nothing is sold.

          In progress

        raW will not sell identifiable responses. In the app, answers are shown to other members only as totals. Any insight product is opt-in and built for the person who answered.
