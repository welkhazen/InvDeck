# Research sources, methods and claim audit

_Compiled 16 October 2026. Every number in `index.html` appears below with its source, date, unit and caveats._

## How sources were checked

- **External figures were checked with web search** against the publisher or press coverage of the publisher's release.
- **This build environment blocks direct connections to most of these hosts** (who.int, worldbank.org, ilo.org, sec.gov, gallup.com, datareportal.com and others).
  - So **no page was opened directly**, and the links were not click-tested from here.
  - Every URL listed did come back as a live search result.
  - **Before sending the deck, the founder should click every link once.**
- **Internal figures** were read directly from the raW repositories (`therawme`, `wzwznew`) at their October 2026 state.
- **Status values:**
  - **Verified (secondary)**: the figure matches the publisher's release as reported in search results, and at least two independent summaries agree.
  - **Verified (code)**: read directly from raW source code or shipped data.
  - **Founder-reported**: supplied by the founder or in the founder's documents; source files were not available.
  - **Assumption**: an illustrative input, labeled as such in the deck.

## Qualitative competitor statements

Statements about Instagram, TikTok, Discord, Yubo, Wizz, Secret, Yik Yak, NGL, 16Personalities, Boo, Character.AI and Replika are kept qualitative.

- **Yubo's model** (in-app purchases and subscriptions, no ads) is the company's own description on its careers page. Third-party revenue estimates conflict by a factor of several, so no Yubo figure is used.
- **No claim is made that no competitor does anything similar.**

## Distinctions the deck keeps

- **Loneliness** (a feeling) vs **isolation** (few connections) vs **clinical illness**.
- **Screening results** (probable depression) vs **diagnoses**.
- **Prevalence** (rates) vs **case counts**. Only rates are used.
- **Correlation vs causation.** No indicator is presented as caused by social media, or as something raW would change.
- **Historical measurements vs forecasts.** There are no forecasts. The calculator is labeled arithmetic on assumptions.
