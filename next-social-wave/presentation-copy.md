## 02 · The human problem

**We have never been more connected. Yet millions still feel alone.**

**1 in 6 people worldwide experience loneliness** (WHO Commission on Social Connection, 15.8%, data 2014–2023).[1]
- **17–21%** of people aged 13 to 29 feel lonely, the highest of any age group, with teenagers highest of all.[1]
- **24% vs 11%:** loneliness in low-income countries compared with high-income countries.[1]
- **≈871,000** deaths a year are associated with loneliness, about 100 every hour. This is a modeled estimate.[1]
- **68%** of the world was online in 2024. Access to each other is no longer the gap.[8]

*Evidence card:* regional and income breakdowns, definitions, and Gallup's separate measure (23% lonely "a lot of the day yesterday", 2023).[2] raW is a social discovery product. It is not therapy, a medical service or a treatment.

## 03 · Why Lebanon, why now

**In Lebanon, connection isn't an abstract need.**

Since 2019, Lebanon has lived through a financial collapse, the 2020 Beirut port explosion, a pandemic and war. People stayed online. Many of the structures around them did not hold.

- **Poverty:** 12% (2012) → 44% (2022).[4]
- **Unemployment:** 11.4% → 29.6%. **Youth unemployment:** 23% → 47.8% (2018–19 → January 2022).[5]
- **Screening, 2022:** 47.8% probable depression, 45.3% probable anxiety, 43.5% probable PTSD. These are screening results, not diagnoses. The survey predates the 2024 war.[3]
- **145th of 147** countries for life evaluation (2022–2024).[9]
- **91.6%** of people in Lebanon use the internet.[10]

## 04 · The broken social experience

**Social media made us visible. It didn't always make us understood.**

| The familiar loop | raW's proposed loop |
|---|---|
| Post → Scroll → Compare → Repeat | Answer → Relate → Connect → Contribute → Return |
| Built around what people show. | Built around what people think. Every turn starts with a question and ends with a person. |

**From raW's own history:** asked "Social media: good, or bad?", about two in three of 234 labeled answerers leaned "Bad". This is a model estimate from raW's audience, not a representative survey.

**This is a design hypothesis.** raW has not yet measured that its loop retains people better than a feed. Proving it is what this round pays for.

**Who already serves this need:**
- Instagram and TikTok cover expression.
- Reddit proves pseudonymous communities scale: 101.7M daily active uniques.[11]
- Discord covers live chat and identity.
- Yubo and Wizz cover social discovery.
- Secret, Yik Yak and NGL proved demand for anonymous expression, and struggled with moderation.
- 16Personalities and Boo cover self-discovery.
- Character.AI and Replika offer always-on conversation.

**raW's specific difference:** the room is found through your own honest answers, under a pseudonym, in a moderated space.

## 06 · Interactive investor experience

**Don't just hear about raW. Experience it.**

The investor answers five real raW questions. The real Tribe Test model updates its belief across eight data-defined tribes after every answer. The demo shows:
- the leading tribe,
- the runner-up,
- the remaining uncertainty,
- a trace of how the model's belief moved.

After each answer it also shows how the labeled community leaned, as a model estimate. The investor can continue to 10 questions, then to the full 23.

**Keep in mind (always on screen):**
- The bars show the model's belief, not accuracy or a diagnosis.
- The tribes are clusters in 1,795 people's answers, not validated personality types.
- The full test recovers a voter's data-defined tribe 87.9% of the time in an internal simulation on 1,780 voters (chance is 12.5%). The demo's curated 38-question pool does not inherit that figure.
- Nothing is stored or sent.

## 08 · The Cumulative Mind

**raW didn't start with an algorithm. It started with people answering honestly.**

From story poll to model, with the verification status of each step:

| Step | Figure | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Story polls | ~11K followers; up to ~3,000 votes on one question | Founder-reported |
| 2. Extraction | 33,486 confirmed votes; usernames kept in a private vault | Verified in code |
| 3. Audit | 105 poll instances, 95 trusted, 37,302 trusted user-vote records, 10 pending | Founder-reported |
| 4. Bridge | 31 polls; 23,424 answers added to the model | Verified in code |
| 5. Tribes | 1,795 voters with at least 8 answers → 8 tribes | Verified in code |
| 6. Tribe Test | 87.9% recovery on 1,780 voters | Internal simulation |

- **Measured:** a working adaptive model with documented limits.
- **Suggested:** answers form stable clusters, and a short adaptive test can place a new person near one of them.
- **Not yet verified:** whether the clusters predict who people enjoy talking to, or improve retention.

**The counts are different stages, not a sum.** They are vote records, not people. Data rights to the Instagram-derived answers are on the legal checklist.

**On defensibility.** A dataset alone is not a moat. The potential moat is a retained community that keeps answering.

## 10 · How the model can become a business

**Monetization follows meaningful participation.**

1. **Free participation.** Live and free.
2. **Premium identity.** Built and priced in tokens; buying tokens with money is not live.
3. **Private and paid rooms.** A revenue hypothesis; the app shows a price, but no payment is taken.
4. **Opt-in insights.** Planned.

**raW will not sell identifiable responses.**

*Transparent arithmetic (illustrative):* Lebanon's 5.34M internet users[10] × share who join × share who pay × yearly spend. The default assumptions (1%, 4%, $24) give about 53,400 members, 2,136 paying and about $51K a year. That is a proving ground, not the business case.

Only 23% of adults in Lebanon have an account,[12] so app-store billing and markets beyond Lebanon matter. Reddit's roughly $1.3B in 2024 revenue[11] shows the category can be large. It says nothing about raW's outcome.

## Likely investor objections and short answers

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Anonymous apps always turn toxic." | raW is pseudonymous, not anonymous: accounts, invite codes, 18+, reports, timeouts, bans and blocked words already ship. Safety is the first budget line. |
| "Where's traction?" | Not shown, because it isn't verified. The round buys the baseline and cohort retention within the first months. |
| "Is 87.9% real?" | It is real as defined: internal recovery of data-defined tribes in a simulation. It is not psychometric validity, and the deck says so. |
| "Do you own the Instagram data?" | Usernames never leave a private vault. Rights to use the answers are on the legal checklist this round funds. |
| "Lebanon can't pay." | 23% of adults have an account. Revenue tests run through app-store billing, and stage 2 targets markets where payment works. |
| "Isn't this Reddit or Discord?" | They organize around topics you search for or links you have. raW organizes around what you answer, and routes you to the room. |
| "Why will people come back?" | The hypothesis is the conversation, not the feed. Notifications that open the exact conversation are in progress, and return is measured per cohort. |
| "Why is $100K enough?" | It funds a lean Lebanon team for about 12 months to produce evidence, not scale. The $150K and $250K scenarios show what more buys. |
